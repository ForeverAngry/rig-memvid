# UAT: `principal_binding_consistency` v1

## Intent

Verify that a **user-stated preference** persisted via demotion is
retrieved and applied consistently in a later, semantically-related
turn — even when the later turn is phrased differently than the
original statement.

This pins the contract: demoted user turns are not just stored but
remain useful, retrievable, and applicable to follow-up reasoning.

## Setup

- Token budget on `CompactingMemory` ≤ **2,000 tokens**.
- Fresh archive path.
- The system preamble must not encode the preference being tested.

## Script (run verbatim)

### Turn 1 — user states the preference

```text
For future reference: I'm vegetarian, I don't drink alcohol, and I
strongly prefer trains over flying for any trip under 8 hours.
```

### Turns 2–10 — filler (≥ 80 words each)

Run 9 filler turns on topics unrelated to food, drink, or travel so
the preference statement is evicted. Suggested topics: same as the
other UAT specs (container orchestration, Bayeux Tapestry, Bach
partitas, …).

### Turn 11 — probe (food)

```text
User: Recommend a celebration dinner for tomorrow night, somewhere
nice.
```

### Turn 12 — probe (drink, separate query)

```text
User: What should I pair the meal with?
```

### Turn 13 — probe (travel, indirect)

```text
User: I need to be in Paris from Amsterdam on the 14th. Best way to
get there?
```

### Turn 14 — direct memory check

```text
User: List everything you know about my preferences.
```

## Acceptance criteria

| # | Criterion                                                              | Pass condition                                                                                                       |
| - | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 | The food recommendation respects vegetarianism                         | Turn 11 reply does not recommend a meat or fish dish as the main, and does not require omitting an animal protein.   |
| 2 | The drink pairing respects no-alcohol                                  | Turn 12 reply suggests a non-alcoholic pairing, or explicitly says "I remember you don't drink — here's a non-alcoholic pairing". |
| 3 | The travel suggestion respects train preference                        | Turn 13 reply leads with rail (Thalys / Eurostar / Amsterdam-Paris direct). Mentioning a flight as a fallback is OK; recommending a flight as the primary choice is not. |
| 4 | The direct preferences query is complete and correct                   | Turn 14 reply lists **all three** preferences (vegetarian, no alcohol, prefer trains under 8 h). No invented preferences added. |
| 5 | No reversed preferences                                                | None of the replies suggest the user **eats meat**, **drinks alcohol**, or **prefers flying** for short hops.        |

## Failure modes to log explicitly

- **F1** — Turn 11 leads with a steak / fish dish.
- **F2** — Turn 12 leads with a wine / cocktail recommendation.
- **F3** — Turn 13 recommends a flight as primary.
- **F4** — Turn 14 misses a preference or invents one (e.g. "you prefer Italian cuisine").
- **F5** — Any reply asserts the opposite of a stored preference.

Any of F1–F5 → `FAIL`. Score the failure count in the result file so
trends can be compared across versions.
